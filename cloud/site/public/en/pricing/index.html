Common questions

Is there any difference between monthly and annual?
Only the price and the billing cycle. Annual is 31% cheaper; monthly can be stopped from next month.
Which devices can I use it on?
Today, the Windows app. Mac, iPhone, and Android apps are in the works and will be covered by the same subscription at no extra cost. There is no device limit.
Who processes the payment?
Paddle, as Merchant of Record. Paddle issues the receipt and handles tax; Daynote never receives your card details.
What happens when the trial ends?
Only the syncing stops. No card is on file, so nothing is charged, and both the notes on your PC and the copy already uploaded stay put.
Does cancelling delete my notes?
No. Cancelling only prevents the next renewal, and syncing continues until the period you paid for ends. See the refund policy.
Can I switch from monthly to annual?
Yes, in the Paddle customer portal opened from Settings → Account in the app. The unused part of the monthly period is prorated.
